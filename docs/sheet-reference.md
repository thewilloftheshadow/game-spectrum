## Scoring model

Formula from the sheet:

```txt
(Total 100 - Total 35 + Replayability) * Extra %
```

Main categories:

- Mechanics - 25
    - Fun/Feeling - 10
    - Immersive - 10
    - Variety - 5
- Visuals - 20
    - Artistry - 15
    - UI - 5
- Personality - 30
    - Narration Theme - 15
    - Authenticity - 5
    - Originality - 10
- Audio - 10
    - Music - 5
    - Effects/Vocals - 5
- Control - 15
    - Interface - 5
    - Control - 5
    - Learning Curve - 5
- Penalties - 35
    - Performance - 20
    - Bad Moment - 5
    - Inconsistency - 10
- Bonus
    - Replayability - 10
    - Extra % MAX = 1.1%
